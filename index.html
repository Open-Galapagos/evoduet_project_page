01 / Abstract

Opening the
discovery loop.

Search⇄Solve

Evolutionary search with large language models (LLMs) can stall when progress requires external knowledge the model lacks. Supplying relevant documents helps, but simply adding a web search tool can keep returning the same pages as solutions change.

We introduce EvoDuet, a bilevel optimization method that co-evolves solutions and search queries with fixed model parameters. At each iteration, a retrieval gate lets the LLM assess its knowledge gap and choose to retrieve new documents, reuse stored ones, or proceed without them. An inner loop refines queries and ranks documents by the solution scores they are predicted to yield; an outer loop generates candidates in parallel from these documents and records the evaluated outcomes for later searches.

Across 21 optimization tasks with one candidate per iteration, EvoDuet raises OpenEvolve’s normalized discovery gain from 74.1% to 78.0% with GPT-5.6-Luna and from 61.3% to 82.3% with Gemini-3.8-Flash, whereas Qwen3.5-9B does not benefit. Our best runs surpass the previously reported best scores on eight tasks, including Swap Reduction on Q20 and Rosetta, and match them on three more. EvoDuet also improves with other scaffolds, including Top-K and EvoX, on Sums/Diffs and Denoising.

02 / Method

A duet of searching and solving.

The model’s parameters stay fixed. Its solutions, queries, and knowledge state evolve together.

01 — Outer loop

Evolve the solution

Select a parent, generate candidates, and evaluate them. Retain the best valid candidate and store the evidence with its measured outcome.

02 — Retrieval gate

Assess the knowledge gap

Decide whether the next revision needs new web evidence, can reuse stored documents, or can proceed with the model’s existing knowledge.

03 — Inner loop

Evolve the search

Refine queries and rank documents by the candidate scores they are predicted to enable, before spending the budget to generate candidates.

When should an agent search?

Explore the three decisions at each iteration.

Find new evidence. A knowledge gap remains unresolved. The inner loop searches the web and refines its queries; the outer loop generates candidates from the retained documents.

05 / A closer look

How does web knowledge help?

55 / 82 runs

Transferring methods into solutions

The most common behavior is applying a retrieved principle or method. Reusing public artifacts is less common: it appears in 6 of 82 audited GPT-5.6-Luna runs.

6.9× lower estimated cost

Going further on Denoising

EvoDuet reaches 100.27% NDG at $38.45, compared with SimpleTES at 100% NDG and an estimated API-equivalent cost of $265.39.

A loop that fits other loops

EvoDuet also improves OpenEvolve, Top-K, and EvoX on Sums/Diffs and Denoising. The search loop can work alongside existing solution and strategy loops.
