Abstract

EvoDuet co-evolves solutions and web search queries for scientific discovery, with fixed model parameters. A retrieval gate chooses between new searches, stored documents, and existing knowledge. Queries are refined using predicted solution scores; evaluated candidates inform later searches. Across 21 optimization tasks, EvoDuet improves discovery with GPT-5.6-Luna and Gemini-3.8-Flash, but not Qwen3.5-9B. Selected best runs improve on eight previously reported scores and match three.
